The next feature to add to farm generation is "outlands". Outlands are a simple tesselation of a geographic extension of the inner rectangular farm domain (which is tesselated via T-tesselation). The outlands are very simple: extend the farm geography by outlands_m in both dimensions. Tesselate the outlands into 4 rectangles, which are formed by horizontally extending the north and south bounding irrigation channels of the inner rectangular farm to the left and right until they intersect the outer bounds of the outlands domain. This divides the headlands into 4 rectangular fields, outlands_north, outlands_south, outlands_east and outlands_west. The outlands are populated with irrigation channels, just as the inner farm is. The distinction for the outlands is that no roads are present - only irrigation channels at field boundaries - just as in the inner farm. This means that all channels have T-intersections, just as they do in the inner farm. Furthermore, we also still have a perimeter irrigation channel (or 1/2 of one) at the outer edge of the outlands, just as we did for the inner farm. Since outlands fields are rectangular, we can populate them with crops in the same way we did for the inner farm.

The task is to add the outlands fields and populate them with the trees and weeds as used in the inner farm. We will need to generate new GIS semantic data so that the names and nature of these fields are provided in the vector layer. These new outland fields will be of type "crop", but we need to extend the farm IR to include something that indicates, for example, ParcelGroup: for example, outland or farm. The road network code (generation/roads.py) will need to be updated to loop over parcels which are not in ParcelGroup=outland. 

The generation of surface height and friction should proceed as before, but the domain of definition of the global friction and height variations should extend out to the edges of the new domain - which includes outlands. The origin of the GIS system can also be moved to the outer corner of the outlands.

If the new FarmGenerationConfig parameter outlands_m is less than or equal to zero, no outlands will be generated - all code should behave as before.

Once the 60mx60m plots look correct, we shall move on to check usda and gis outputs - although if these are being generated by the current code structure, that is OK. All new test outputs (pngs, etc) should contain "outlands" in the filename.
